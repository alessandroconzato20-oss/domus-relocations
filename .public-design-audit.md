# Public Design Audit

The services page is the visual benchmark: charcoal, parchment, gold, serif led hierarchy, thin rules, and restrained editorial layouts. The homepage remaining sections, International Students page, and Corporate landing page will be refreshed toward this system. DOMUS Compass and DOMUS Meridian must remain unchanged. The mobile intake and login flows already read as appropriately quiet and on brand.

The refresh should replace generic feature card grids with numbered rows, asymmetrical composition, photographic or material moments where relevant, and gold as the sole accent color.
